# Runbook: Site Power Failure

## Battery and DG
Most sites run on battery for about 4 hours. The diesel generator (DG) should start automatically when mains fails.

## Escalation
If battery is below 20 percent and DG has not started, dispatch the field team with fuel and escalate per ESC-901.
