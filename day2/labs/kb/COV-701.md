# 5G Coverage and Eligibility FAQ

## Who gets 5G
5G is included in Postpaid Max 599 and in prepaid recharges of Rs 349 and above. You also need a 5G phone.

## Where is 5G live
NetNova 5G is live in Chennai, Mumbai, Delhi, Bengaluru and Hyderabad. More cities are added every quarter.
