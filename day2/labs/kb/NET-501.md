# Slow Data Troubleshooting for Customers

## Quick checks
Restart the phone and toggle airplane mode for 10 seconds. Check that the APN is set to netnova.internet. Make sure the daily data limit has not been used up.

## Still slow
Move to an area with better signal and try again. If the problem continues, raise a complaint in the app with your location and time.
