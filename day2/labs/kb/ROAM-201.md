# International Roaming Packs for Postpaid

## Eligibility
Postpaid customers with 3 months of on-time payments need no deposit. Activate the pack in the NetNova app at least 2 hours before travel.

## Packs and prices
1-day pack: Rs 799 for 1 GB and 100 minutes. 10-day pack: Rs 2,999 for 5 GB and 100 minutes. 30-day pack: Rs 5,999 for 15 GB and 300 minutes.

## Billing
Pack charges are added to your next bill. Usage beyond the pack is charged at pay-per-use rates shown in the app.
