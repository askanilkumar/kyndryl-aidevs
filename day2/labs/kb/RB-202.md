# Runbook: Backhaul Fibre Cut

## Confirm the cut
Confirm LOS on the aggregation router port. Look up the link ID in ASSET-801 to find the owner and route.

## Restore service
Notify the fibre owner NOC. Switch affected sites to microwave backup where available; this should happen within 10 minutes.

## Repair and close
Splicing normally takes 4 to 6 hours. Raise a P1 if 10 or more sites are affected. Update the ticket every 30 minutes.
