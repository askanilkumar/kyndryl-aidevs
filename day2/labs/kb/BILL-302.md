# Billing and Payment Error Codes

## E-4107
E-4107 means the payment gateway timed out. The money may be debited but the recharge or bill payment is not confirmed. If debited, it is automatically reversed within 3 working days.

## E-4120
E-4120 means a duplicate payment was detected. The second payment is refunded within 5 working days.

## E-5003
E-5003 means your UPI AutoPay mandate has expired. Register the mandate again in the app under Payments.
