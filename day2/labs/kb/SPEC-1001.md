# 3GPP Notes: 5QI Quality Classes

## What 5QI is
5QI is a number that tells the 5G network what quality a data flow needs. These are study notes summarised from the 3GPP QoS framework.

## Common values
5QI 1 is conversational voice and is a guaranteed bit rate flow. 5QI 5 is IMS signalling. 5QI 9 is the default best effort flow for normal internet data.
