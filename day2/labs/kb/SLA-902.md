# Network Incident Severity and SLA

## Severity levels
P1: 10 or more sites down, or any core outage. NOC must respond within 15 minutes and update every 30 minutes.
P2: 3 to 9 sites down. Respond within 30 minutes and update every hour.
P3: 1 to 2 sites down. Respond within 4 hours.
