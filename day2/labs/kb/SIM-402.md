# SIM Swap Security Rules

## Restrictions after a swap
After any SIM swap or eSIM change, outgoing and incoming SMS are blocked for 24 hours to protect your bank OTPs.

## If you did not request a swap
Call 198 from another phone immediately. NetNova will lock the number and restore it after identity verification.
