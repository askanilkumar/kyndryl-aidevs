# Runbook: Cell Site Down

## Triage
Open the alarm list for the site. If the alarm says Mains Fail, follow RB-203. If the alarm says LOS or Transport Down, follow RB-202.

## Remote action
If no clear alarm, ping the OAM address and reset the baseband once. Do not reset more than once.

## Dispatch
If the site does not recover in 15 minutes, dispatch the field team and raise a ticket. Set severity as per SLA-902.
