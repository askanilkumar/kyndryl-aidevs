# eSIM Activation and SIM Replacement

## eSIM
You need an eSIM-capable phone. Request eSIM in the app, complete video KYC and scan the QR code within 2 hours. The QR code works only once.

## Physical SIM replacement
Visit a NetNova store with an ID proof. A replacement SIM is active within 2 hours of KYC completion.
