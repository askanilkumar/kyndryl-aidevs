# VoLTE and Wi-Fi Calling Setup

## VoLTE
Turn on VoLTE in the mobile network settings. It needs a 4G or 5G supported phone and a NetNova SIM.

## Wi-Fi calling
Wi-Fi calling works on supported phones and is free of extra charge. Use it when mobile signal is weak indoors.
