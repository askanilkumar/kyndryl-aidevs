# Prepaid Smart 299

## Price and validity
Prepaid Smart 299 costs Rs 299 and is valid for 28 days.

## Data and calls
You get 1.5 GB of data per day, unlimited voice calls and 100 SMS per day. After the daily data limit, speed drops to 64 kbps until midnight.

## 5G
This plan does not include 5G. Unlimited 5G needs a recharge of Rs 349 or above.
