# Postpaid Max 599

## Price and validity
Postpaid Max 599 costs Rs 599 per month plus GST. The bill cycle starts on the activation date. There is no lock-in period.

## Data and calls
The plan includes 100 GB of data every month. Unused data rolls over up to 200 GB. Voice calls are unlimited and 100 SMS per day are included.

## 5G and extras
Unlimited 5G is included at no extra cost in all NetNova 5G cities. The plan includes NetNova TV and one more OTT app of your choice. Family add-on SIMs cost Rs 249 per month each, up to 3 members.
