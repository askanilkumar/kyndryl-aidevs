# Incident INC-4502: Prepaid Recharge Failures

## Summary
On 3 October 2026, prepaid recharges failed for about 2 hours with error E-4107. Severity was P2.

## Cause and fix
The primary payment gateway vendor was timing out. Traffic was moved to the secondary gateway and recharges recovered. Customers who were debited were reversed automatically.
