# Incident INC-4471: Chennai North Outage

## Summary
On 12 September 2026 at 02:10 IST, 14 sites in Chennai North went down. Severity was P1.

## Cause
Alarms showed LOS on link FB-CHN-07. A road excavation near Ambattur junction cut the fibre.

## Action
The team followed RB-202. Microwave backup restored 9 sites in 20 minutes. The fibre was spliced at 07:40 and the ticket was closed.
