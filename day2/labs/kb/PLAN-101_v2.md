# Postpaid Max 499 (old version)

## Price and validity
Postpaid Max costs Rs 499 per month plus GST. This version was replaced on 1 August 2026.

## Data and calls
The plan includes 75 GB of data every month with no rollover. Voice calls are unlimited and 100 SMS per day are included.

## 5G and extras
5G access is charged at Rs 99 extra per month. Family add-on SIMs cost Rs 299 per month each.
