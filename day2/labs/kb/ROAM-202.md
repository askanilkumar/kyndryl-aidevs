# International Roaming Packs for Prepaid

## Eligibility
Prepaid customers need an active plan of Rs 599 or above with at least 28 days of validity left. Activate the pack in the NetNova app at least 4 hours before travel.

## Packs and prices
1-day pack: Rs 899 for 1 GB and 100 minutes. 10-day pack: Rs 3,499 for 5 GB and 100 minutes. 30-day pack: Rs 6,499 for 15 GB and 300 minutes.

## Billing
The pack price is deducted from your prepaid balance at activation. If the balance is low, activation fails.
