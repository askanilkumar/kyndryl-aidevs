# Bill Dispute and Refund Policy

## How to dispute
Raise a dispute in the app or at a NetNova store within 60 days of the bill date. Attach the bill number and a short reason.

## Refund timeline
Approved refunds are paid to the original payment mode within 7 working days.

## Large disputes
Disputes above Rs 5,000 are reviewed by the Level 2 billing team and can take up to 15 working days.
