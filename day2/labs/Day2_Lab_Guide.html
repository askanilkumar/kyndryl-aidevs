The storyNetNova Telecom is an Indian mobile operator. Customers ask about plans, roaming, bills and SIMs. Engineers in the network operations centre (NOC) ask about runbooks and incidents. Your job today: build an assistant that answers from the knowledge base, and make it trustworthy.

Failure Lab · last 30 min

Break it, find it, fix it

Prove each fix with the Evals numbers or with a before and after answer. Keep a note for your demo.

  1. Bad chunks. Set Chunking to fixed and the chunk size slider to 10 words. Ask about Postpaid Max data. What breaks? Find the size where it works again, then compare with section chunks.
  2. Stale index. Open kb\PLAN-101.md and change Rs 599 to Rs 649. Save. Ask the price in the app. Is the answer new? Click Re-index from kb/ folder in the sidebar and ask again. Change the file back afterwards.
  3. Leaky assistant. Pick the Baseline preset and a customer role. Ask three questions that should be blocked. Which single switch closes the leak?
