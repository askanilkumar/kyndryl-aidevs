2 · Hybrid search: meaning plus exact words

Query: "What does error code E-4107 mean?" Vector search understands meaning but blurs exact codes. Keyword search (BM25) nails exact strings but misses paraphrases. Hybrid fuses both lists with reciprocal rank fusion: score = sum of 1 / (60 + rank).

Why fuse ranks, not scoresVector scores (0 to 1) and BM25 scores (0 to 20) are on different scales. Ranks are comparable. Illustrative lists, same idea as Lab 4.

5 · Evals: prove the lift with numbers

What the Evals tab measures

  • retrieval_hit: was the right document in the evidence? (higher is better)
  • answer_correct: does the answer contain the fact? For unanswerable questions, refusing is correct. (higher)
  • stale_rate: an old article reached the model (lower)
  • leak_rate: a document the user must not see reached the model (lower)
  • avg_seconds, total_usd: the price of the improvement

A good test question has

  • one clear answer inside one article
  • a short phrase (the key) that must appear in a correct answer
  • a role: who is asking
  • words that differ a little from the article, so retrieval has to work
