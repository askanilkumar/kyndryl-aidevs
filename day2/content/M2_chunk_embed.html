Module 2 · Chunks and vectors

Cut it well, then turn meaning into numbers

Two decisions decide most of your RAG quality: how you cut documents, and how you search the pieces. Try both here before you code them.

1 · Chunking: where you cut matters

This is the real PLAN-101 article. Question: "How much data does Postpaid Max include?" Move the slider and watch the chunk that holds the answer.

What the model would see

Rule of thumbCut where the author cut: by heading. Put the document title inside every chunk so a piece never loses its subject.

2 · Embeddings: same meaning, different words

An embedding turns text into a list of numbers. Texts with similar meaning get similar numbers. Teaching toy: here each text has only 6 hand-set numbers (topics). Real models use about 1,500. The idea is identical.

Keyword search sees

Read the result

  • The bar is cosine similarity: 1.00 means the same direction, 0 means unrelated.
  • Pick "How many gigabytes on my top plan?". It shares no important word with "100 GB", yet it still finds the plan.
  • Pick "vegan food Fridays". Everything is near zero.
  • Notice the old PLAN-101 v2 scores almost the same as v3. Meaning cannot tell old from new. Metadata can.

3 · The vector database: nearest chunks, with filters and a floor
