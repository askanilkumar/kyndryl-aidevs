1 · The model alone vs the model with your document

Same model, same question. Tap to switch.

Question from a NetNova customer

How much data does Postpaid Max include?

Three reasons the model alone fails

  • Private: it never saw NetNova's price list.
  • Old: it was trained months ago. Plans change.
  • No proof: it cannot show where the answer came from.

RAG fixes all three: fetch the current page, give it to the model, ask for a cited answer.

3 · The five parts you will build

1 Ingest

Load documents

Read files and keep their metadata: who may see it, which version, current or old.

Goes wrong: old versions sit next to new ones.

2 Chunk

Cut into pieces

Pieces small enough to be precise, big enough to make sense on their own.

Goes wrong: a cut in the middle of the answer.

3 Embed + store

Numbers in a vector database

Each chunk becomes a list of numbers that captures its meaning. Qdrant stores them.

Goes wrong: you change a document and forget to re-index.

4 Retrieve

Find the best chunks

Turn the question into numbers, find the nearest chunks, apply filters.

Goes wrong: right idea, wrong words; or the wrong audience.

5 Generate

Answer from the evidence

Send the chunks and the question to the model. Ask for citations. Allow "I don't know".

Goes wrong: the model invents, or obeys text hidden in a document.

5 · Day 1 recap quiz

Five questions. Answer before we discuss. Nobody is graded.

Why do we put a LiteLLM gateway in front of the models?

WhyA gateway gives one stable address and keys per person, and can switch to a fallback model when one fails.

A model weight stored in 16 bits is quantized to 4 bits. What is the main effect?

WhyFewer bits per weight means less memory, so a bigger model fits on the same GPU, with a small quality cost.

What grows with every token of a long conversation and eats GPU memory?

WhyThe KV cache stores attention keys and values for every token so far.

Your model must answer from a policy that changes weekly. Best fit?

WhyFacts that change belong in a retrieval index you can update, not in the weights.

Why give each participant a virtual key instead of the real cloud key?

WhyPer-person keys give control and safety: limit, track and revoke individually.
