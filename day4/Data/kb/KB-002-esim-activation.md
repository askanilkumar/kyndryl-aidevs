# KB-002 — Activating a NetNova eSIM

To activate an eSIM you need: the QR code emailed to the registered address, an active internet connection on the new device, and the device must support eSIM.

Steps:
1. Settings → Mobile → Add eSIM → Scan QR code.
2. Wait up to 5 minutes for the carrier profile to download.
3. Restart the phone once.

QR codes expire 72 hours after issue. If expired, raise a ticket under category eSIM and we re-issue it within 2 working hours.
