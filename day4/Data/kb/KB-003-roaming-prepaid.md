# KB-003 — International roaming — Prepaid Smart plan

Prepaid Smart does NOT include international roaming by default.

To enable roaming:
1. Open MyNet app → Services → International roaming.
2. Pick a day/week/month pack and pay from prepaid balance.
3. Roaming activates within 15 minutes.

Supported countries: UAE, Singapore, UK, USA, Australia. Other countries are not supported on Prepaid Smart.

For Postpaid plans see KB-004.
