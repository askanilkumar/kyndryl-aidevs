# KB-004 — International roaming — Postpaid plans (Gold / Platinum)

Postpaid Gold and Platinum include free roaming in SAARC countries. For other countries, a daily pack is auto-applied on the first use of data/voice abroad.

Daily pack costs:
- Zone 1 (UAE, Singapore, UK): INR 599/day
- Zone 2 (USA, Canada, Australia, EU): INR 749/day
- Zone 3 (rest of world): INR 999/day

To stop charges, switch off mobile data on the device. Charges stop at the next midnight local time.

Platinum VIP customers must be routed to a human agent for any roaming change (see KB-018).
