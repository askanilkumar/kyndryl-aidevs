Why audit = eval source

You don't need a separate "eval dataset" to measure your agent. The audit log already tells you:

  • Denial rate per role — tightness of the registry.
  • Approval latency — human bottlenecks.
  • Rejection rate — how often the agent proposes bad calls.
  • Error rate per tool — reliability of the MCP server.
Rule of thumb: if an action has any customer or money impact, it should at minimum be proposed → approved. Reads can be free; writes should pause.
