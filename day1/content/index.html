Rules of the room

  • Predict, then measure. Write your guess before you run anything.
  • Stuck for 10 minutes? Raise a hand with the exact error and what you tried.
  • Your key is yours. Never paste it into chat, Git or a screenshot.
  • No raw cloud keys on any VM. Everything goes through a gateway.
  • Keep NOTES.md. One line per lab is enough.
